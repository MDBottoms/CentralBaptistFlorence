Meet Our Staff

Get to know the people who serve and lead at Central Baptist Church

Todd Wilson

Pastor

I've been blessed to call the Shoals area home for most of my life. My wife, Amelia, and I have been married for 19 years, and together we're raising four energetic and wonderful children — Jay, Zavien, Daniel, and Madelyn.

After graduating from Lexington High School, I attended the University of North Alabama, where I had the opportunity to play football under Coach Bill Hyde. Following graduation in 2003, I began a career in home finance, working with Wells Fargo Financial before moving into banking with Regions Bank in 2006. In 2021, I joined First Southern Bank. Throughout my career, God has blessed me with incredible teams, and I'm grateful for the opportunity to make a difference in people's lives each day.

From an early age, I've felt God calling me to serve Him. While I didn't always understand what that would look like, I've sought to follow Him and grow in His grace along the way. Through life's challenges, God has continued to deepen my faith and guide me in walking closely with Him.

Today, my heart is to help others build a strong foundation of faith — especially in a world that often pulls us in the opposite direction. Our desire as a church is to grow closer to God and to share His love with those around us, so that when life's challenges come, we can stand firm in Him.

It is truly a blessing to serve as Pastor here and to experience the love and joy of this church family every day. I'm excited about what God is doing and where He is leading us.

Tiffany Wells

Kids Director

Tiffany recently joined our church family as our Children's Ministry Director. Originally from the West, she made Alabama home in 2001, where she met her husband, Mark. Together, they are raising two teenagers, Lyla and Atlas.

She has a heart for creating a safe, nurturing environment where children can grow in their faith and feel loved and known. Her passion is to come alongside families, helping equip children with a strong foundation in Christ that will guide them as they grow.
